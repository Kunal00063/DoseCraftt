Comprehensive Treatment Planning Services

Delivering precision, efficiency, and clinical excellence for radiation oncology departments worldwide.

IMRT & VMAT Planning

Our dosimetrists excel in creating highly conformal dose distributions using Advanced Intensity-Modulated Radiation Therapy (IMRT) and Volumetric Modulated Arc Therapy (VMAT). We focus on maximizing target coverage while minimizing dose to critical organs at risk (OARs), resulting in superior clinical outcomes and reduced toxicity.

  • Complex head and neck, prostate, and breast cases
  • RapidArc® & VMAT optimization for faster delivery times
  • Strict adherence to RTOG protocols and physician directives

SRS & SBRT Planning

Stereotactic Radiosurgery (SRS) and Stereotactic Body Radiotherapy (SBRT) demand absolute precision. Our team utilizes advanced imaging fusion and sub-millimeter planning techniques to deliver ablative doses to small targets while achieving rapid dose fall-off to protect surrounding healthy tissue.

  • Brain metastases, lung, spine, and liver lesions
  • Integration with advanced motion management systems
  • Rigorous independent dose calculation and QA verification

3D Conformal Radiotherapy

For cases requiring robust, reliable treatment delivery, our 3D Conformal Radiotherapy (3D-CRT) planning utilizes CT-based 3D anatomical models. We carefully select beam angles and employ virtual wedges or field-in-field techniques to achieve homogeneous dose coverage.

  • Palliative treatments, whole brain, and uncomplicated bony metastases
  • Forward planning techniques (Field-in-Field) to reduce hot spots
  • Electron block design and verification

Emergency STAT Planning

Medical emergencies don't clock out. DoseCraftt offers priority, expedited treatment planning for critical cases requiring immediate intervention, such as spinal cord compressions or superior vena cava syndrome.

  • 2-4 hour turnaround times for STAT cases
  • Direct line to the assigned dosimetrist/physicist
  • Available 24/7/365 including holidays
